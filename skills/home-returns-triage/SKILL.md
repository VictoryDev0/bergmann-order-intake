# Home returns triage (phase two)

Bergmann Home returns run at 9 % overall and 18 % on the Induct 80 hob. Most
of them cite "doesn't work with my pans" or "error code E3". Customer service
knows this; product management hears it quarterly.

Same pattern as the dealer order intake: a workflow gathers the evidence
(tickets from the service tool, returns from Shopware, warranty status from
SAP), the agent judges and writes, a person decides.

## Weekly brief

1. Group the week's tickets and returns by product and by stated reason.
2. For the top three groups: count, share of that product's sales, trend
   against the previous four weeks, two verbatim customer quotes.
3. One proposed action per group, owner named (product, service, content).
4. Never contact a customer. The brief goes to product management and the
   head of customer service.
